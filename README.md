# CareerPilot AI

10 screens, one persona, built to answer a single question: what would a career operating system actually look like if it wasn't a pile of disconnected tools?

![Type: AI Product](https://img.shields.io/badge/Type-AI%20Product-1F3864) ![Stage: Product Design](https://img.shields.io/badge/Stage-Product%20Design-6B7280)

## The gap I was designing for

Most people manage their career with a fragmented mess — a job board here, a resume tool there, LinkedIn for networking, spreadsheets for tracking applications. Nothing talks to anything else. CareerPilot AI, a product under Unlock AI, is my answer to what a single, coherent operating system for your career journey could look like instead.

## The screens

Dashboard, Career Health Check, Job Discovery, Resume Studio, Auto Apply, LinkedIn Outreach AI, Interview Prep, Concierge, Application Tracker — nine functional screens plus the dashboard, each one a real decision point a job seeker actually faces.

## Design choice: one real persona, not generic placeholders

Every screen is built around a single, specific persona — a VP of Operations based in Delhi NCR — rather than lorem-ipsum content. That constraint forced every interaction, recommendation, and piece of copy to reflect what an actual senior professional would need to see, not a generic demo that looks fine but doesn't hold up under scrutiny.

## My role

Designed the full product end to end — screen flow, information architecture, interaction model — then built the interactive prototype myself for co-founder and product-team review.

## Status

This is a design artifact: a fully interactive prototype, not a shipped production application. Built for internal stakeholder review, which is exactly what it's being used for.
